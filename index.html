About Zeyad Salah

AI/ML Engineer in progress & UI/UX Designer bridging intelligence and interaction.

AI/ML Engineer in progress & UI/UX Designer | Computer Science (AI) student building the systems between an idea that works on paper and one that actually runs, and the Figma interfaces that make it usable.

Startups and product teams often have a working idea that never turns into a working system | a model that runs on someone's laptop but never becomes something a user can touch or an investor can believe. That gap between “it technically works” and “it's actually usable” is where I spend most of my time.

I build the full path between those two points: cleaning and engineering data, training and evaluating models, and turning the result into a deployed, interactive product | including Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems, LLM applications with careful prompt design, and the Figma interfaces that sit on top of them. REVORA, a live Streamlit app that predicts a car's horsepower from its specs, walks that whole path from raw dataset to public tool. The RAG and LLM work I did during my internship at Tips Hindawi followed the same discipline | architecture, evaluation, and something that actually answers real queries. On the design side, EgyTech, SAVORÉ, and SHOPLY show the same instinct applied to product design: complete Figma flows, wireframes, and interactive prototypes following Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, built to be used, not just looked at.

I keep the process visible along the way | short iterations, models tested against real metrics rather than convenient ones, design decisions grounded in user flow rather than guesswork, and documentation clean enough that someone else could pick up exactly where I left off.

If you're sitting on an AI idea | or need the interface that makes it real | my projects above are a good place to start, or email me: ZeyadElshanab@gmail.com
