---

### How to enable if not live yet

1. Go to your repository: [github.com/ZeyadSalah134/portfolio](https://github.com/ZeyadSalah134/portfolio)
2. Click **Settings** (top right of the repo)
3. Scroll down to **Pages** in the left sidebar
4. Under **Branch**, select `main` → `/ (root)` → click **Save**
5. Wait ~1 minute, then your site will be live at the link above

---

### Pinning the link on your repo homepage

1. Go to [github.com/ZeyadSalah134/portfolio](https://github.com/ZeyadSalah134/portfolio)
2. Click the **⚙️ gear icon** next to **"About"** (top right of the repo page)
3. Paste the URL `https://zeyadSalah134.github.io/portfolio` into the **Website** field
4. Click **Save changes**

Now every visitor to your GitHub repo will see the live link right at the top.
